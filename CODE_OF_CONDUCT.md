# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

Magpie is a small project with a small number of people in it, so this doesn't need to be long -
but it does need to be written down, because the moment it matters is the moment nobody wants to
be inventing a policy.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new, or working in a second language.
- Disagree about the work directly and specifically. "This breaks anchoring on paginated pages"
  is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Accept that the answer to a well-made proposal is sometimes still no. Magpie's scope is
  deliberately narrow.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised content, deliberate intimidation,
sustained disruption, or publishing anyone's private information. Trolling and bad-faith argument
count too.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and any other space where you are participating in
the project.

## Enforcement

Report to **raden.dev@pm.me**. Reports stay confidential.

I maintain this alone, so I'm also the one who acts on it. Depending on severity that means a
warning, deleting the content, or blocking the account. I'll try to be proportionate, and I'd
rather resolve something with a conversation than a ban.
