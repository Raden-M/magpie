# Monochrome interface with structural iridescence

Date: 2026-07-23
Status: accepted

## Context

A magpie's blue-green-purple is structural colour: the feather holds no pigment, and the colour
only appears when light catches the structure against a black base. The product thesis is the
same - the value is the shine on the few sentences worth keeping.

## Decision

A true monochrome interface (magpie black, cool white) with iridescence - blue to teal to purple -
as the only colour, spent on highlights and the states that echo them. Serif reading surface, sans
chrome, mono data. Light and dark both first-class.

## Alternatives rejected

A conventional multi-hue palette: generic, and it would put colour everywhere except where it
means something.

## Consequences

A single accent family constrains future categorical colour, such as many highlight categories.
Handled by shade and positional variation within the iridescent range rather than new hues.
